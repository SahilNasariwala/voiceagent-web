VoiceAgent for Android

Download VoiceAgent.apk

2.4 MB · version 0.1.0 · for Android 10+

  1. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  2. Open VoiceAgent and follow its checklist: paste your Groq key, allow the microphone.
  3. Accessibility: tap “Open Accessibility settings” → Installed apps → VoiceAgent → On.
    Greyed out / “Restricted setting”? In the app tap “App settings” → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings, then try again.
  4. Tap “Don't optimise VoiceAgent” so Samsung doesn't put the bubble to sleep.
  5. The 🎙 bubble appears. Drag it anywhere. Open YouTube, play a video, tap the bubble and say “like this video”.
Try: “scroll down” · “go back” · “open Spotify” · “search YouTube for history of Rome” · “tap Subscribe” · “type hello in the comment box”. Taps on Send / Pay / Buy / Delete / Post ask you first.
